## About

I work at the intersection of governance, cybersecurity, human behavior, and AI-enabled systems. My focus is understanding how controls hold up or get bypassed across real organizational contexts.

I study Cyber Defense at UNDEF and Social Psychology at Instituto AMVA, combining technical security, governance, and behavioral analysis.

I build practical tools, assessments, and case studies around GRC, Human Risk, cybersecurity, and AI Security.
